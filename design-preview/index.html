How it works

01A customer reports a problem

They describe it in their own words and get a safe first suggestion.

02A specialist solves it

When the suggestion fails, a person writes the fix and what it applies to.

03The customer confirms

Only after “It worked” does the fix become trusted company knowledge.

04The next customer is helped

A matching problem gets the verified fix, checked against device and safety.
